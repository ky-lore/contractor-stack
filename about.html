Meet the Founder

From the first office chair to the C-suite.

Laura Valdez helped scale NexGen from a local shop into a $100M organization. Now she brings that operational playbook to contractors across the country.

Laura Valdez
$100MScaled at NexGen
Laura Valdez · Operations Strategist

Laura began as the first office employee at NexGen Air Conditioning, Heating & Plumbing — wearing nearly every operational hat imaginable, from CSR and dispatcher to accounting, payroll, purchasing, and HR — eventually serving as Chief Operating Officer.

As the company expanded from a small local operation into a $100M organization, she grew alongside it, implementing the systems, processes, reporting structures, and accountability that supported each stage of growth.

Working alongside her brother Ismael Valdez, NexGen's founder, taught her what it truly takes to build and scale a home-service business — and that sustainable growth isn't created by working harder. It's built through better operations.
